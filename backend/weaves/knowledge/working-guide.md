# Working at Example Co

## Collaboration
Teams use Slack for day-to-day discussion and Linear for tracking planned work.
Urgent customer-impacting incidents are coordinated in the #incident-response
Slack channel. The incident lead posts a short status update every 30 minutes.

## Time off
Employees request planned time off in the People Portal at least five business
days ahead when possible. A manager approves the request. For an unexpected
absence, notify your manager and team in Slack as soon as practical.

## Security
Use the company password manager for credentials. Never paste access tokens or
customer secrets into Slack, tickets, or an AI prompt. Ask Security for access
to restricted customer data.
